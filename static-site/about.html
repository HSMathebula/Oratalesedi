Who We Are

A Legacy of Excellence

Oratalesedi Projects (Pty) Ltd is a 100% black woman-owned company established in 2019. We are dedicated to providing comprehensive solutions across mining services, construction, and renewable energy sectors throughout South Africa.

Our commitment to excellence, safety, and sustainable practices has positioned us as a trusted partner for major industry players, delivering projects valued at over R70 million with a 100% success rate.
